Academic Analysis: Cost Matrix & Trade-Offs

Critical discussion points for university project evaluation and viva examination.

1. Impact of Type I Error (False Positive)

A False Positive occurs when an authentic customer review is mistakenly classified as fake. In e-commerce, this causes censorship of legitimate consumer voices and can penalize honest buyers with account bans. The AdaBoost model reduces False Positives by learning nuanced multi-word combinations rather than splitting abruptly on single keywords.

2. Impact of Type II Error (False Negative)

A False Negative occurs when a fraudulent computer-generated or paid review slips past detection. High False Negative rates degrade trust in platform reviews and artificially elevate deceptive sellers. AdaBoost prioritizes hard-to-classify samples in successive iterations to suppress False Negatives.

3. Why AdaBoost Outperforms Single Decision Trees

A single Decision Tree is susceptible to high variance and overfitting on idiosyncratic vocabulary terms present in the training corpus. AdaBoost combines multiple regularized learners with weighted voting, smoothing the decision boundaries and creating calibrated probability outputs.
