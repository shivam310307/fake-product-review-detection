Prediction Methodology & Signals

How ReviewGuard AI distinguishes authentic sentiment from synthetic text.

1
Decoupled Sentiment vs Authenticity

A positive review is not automatically genuine, and a negative review is not automatically fake. Both positive and negative reviews are evaluated strictly on linguistic markers.

2
Stylometric Feature Extraction

Measures lexical richness (Type-Token Ratio), average sentence length, punctuation frequency (excessive exclamation marks), and n-gram repetitions.

3
Calibrated Class Probabilities

Rather than an opaque binary decision, predictions output exact posterior probabilities \(P(\text{Fake}|\mathbf{x})\) and \(P(\text{Genuine}|\mathbf{x})\).

4
Epistemic Limitation Disclaimer

ReviewGuard AI assesses linguistic deception probability; it does not claim to independently verify physical product authenticity without sensory inspection.
