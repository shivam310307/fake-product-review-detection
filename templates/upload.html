Dataset Schema Requirements

The system automatically maps common column aliases.

Column Status Description
text / review Required The raw text of the customer review.
label / label_binary Required Binary target: 0 (Genuine) or 1 (Fake). Also supports 'CG'/'OR', 'fake'/'real'.
category Optional Product vertical (e.g. Electronics, Books, Beauty, Home).
rating Optional Customer rating (1.0 to 5.0 scale).
vocabulary_richness Computed Type-Token Ratio (TTR) computed automatically during preprocessing.
text_length Computed Character and word counts extracted automatically.
Maximum upload size: 16 MB. Empty rows and unreadable characters will be automatically sanitized.
