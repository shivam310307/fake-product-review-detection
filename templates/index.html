The Core Machine Learning Challenge

Traditional e-commerce platforms confound customer sentiment with review authenticity.

The Fallacy of Sentiment

A positive review is not automatically genuine, and a negative review is not automatically fake. Unethical competitors launch fake negative smear attacks on good products, while dubious vendors purchase fake 5-star praise.

Stylometric & Linguistic Signatures

Computer-generated and incentivized reviews exhibit distinct lexical distributions: inflated hyperbolic adjectives, repetitive vocabulary, abnormal punctuation, and altered syntactic depth compared to real buyers.

Epistemic Limitations

ReviewGuard AI adheres to responsible AI principles: review text alone cannot definitively prove product quality. Predictions reflect linguistic authenticity probabilities with clear uncertainty thresholds.

Base Classifier

Decision Tree Classifier

Constructs an interpretable binary tree using recursive feature splitting. Splitting criteria (Gini Impurity / Information Gain) isolate characteristic word presence across review types.

  • Interpretability: Highly transparent decision rules
  • Splitting: Gini Impurity with max depth regularization
  • Inference Latency: Sub-millisecond execution
  • Feature Importance: Direct Gini importance metrics
Ensemble Classifier

AdaBoost Classifier

Adaptive Boosting algorithm iteratively trains an ensemble of weak base decision stumps. Misclassified samples from earlier rounds receive higher weights, focusing subsequent learners on subtle linguistic deception.

  • Ensemble Method: Sequential adaptive boosting (SAMME)
  • Base Estimator: Regularized Decision Trees
  • Robustness: Significantly reduced variance & overfitting
  • Calibration: Calibrated probability estimates for confidence scoring
